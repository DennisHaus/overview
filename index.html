Entwerfen in der Zeit

Methoden und Konzepte für dynamische Wasserlandschaften der Schweiz

A doctoral research project by Dennis Häusler at Professur Voser, Institute for Landscape and Urban Studies, ETH Zürich.

Swiss water landscapes change over minutes, seasons and decades. Shorelines shift with the water level, alpine slopes creep and break away, and snow and ice hold water back to release it months later. The project develops design methods that work with these changes instead of fixing a single state, through theory, experiments with models, simulations and web tools, and case studies in the plain, the valley and the high Alps.

Places Case study and field sites, on a map. Tools Models, simulations and viewers that run in the browser. Writings Published papers to read online or download as PDF.
